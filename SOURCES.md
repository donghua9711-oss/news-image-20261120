## The eleven drawn stories

1. SHENZHEN DECLARATION (Asia). One Turn: the leaders "met in
   Shenzhen on Thursday and signed a four-point joint
   declaration. It covers reducing nuclear risk, United
   Nations reform, energy supply stability and the
   International Criminal Court's jurisdiction. The text does
   not mention Ukraine or Iran." Open Chronicle: declaration
   "reaffirmed the principle that nuclear war must never be
   fought"; Reuters called it "primarily symbolic". Talks
   lasted roughly 70 minutes (One Turn).
2. KYIV BARRAGE (Europe). CNN, Friday: Russia fired "at least
   110 missiles" overnight into Friday; nine killed, at least
   28 injured; three of Kyiv's Dnipro bridges damaged (Mayor
   Klitschko); Zelensky called it "the biggest this year";
   Poland and NATO scrambled jets and activated Patriot
   systems after airspace violations. Open Chronicle's morning
   edition carried lower early Kyiv tolls (3 dead, 15
   injured); the page uses CNN's later figures.
3. GTA VI SALES (Americas). Reuters, Friday 19:38 UTC:
   "recorded $3.79 billion in retail sales in the first 24
   hours of its launch, developer Rockstar Games said on
   Friday". Business Wire (Take-Two release, Friday): day-one
   retail sales $3.79bn, "the biggest entertainment launch in
   history", 32.5 million units sold pre-release, Metacritic
   99. Sensor Tower's earlier estimate ($2.36bn, digital only)
   was superseded by the company's figure; the page prints
   the company figure, attributed on the card.
4. ICJ / MYANMAR (Europe). Open Chronicle: "On November 19,
   the court found that Myanmar committed genocide against
   the Rohingya during military operations in Rakhine State
   in 2017"; reparations ordered; the UN General Assembly
   president called for Myanmar to facilitate the refugees'
   return. One Turn's Friday brief leads with the ruling:
   "the first such finding against a state by that court."
5. ANTHROPIC CLOSE (Americas). One Turn (North America):
   "Anthropic closes 17% above IPO price in Nasdaq debut."
